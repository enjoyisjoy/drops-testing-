Rules

A short list, so everyone gets a good game.

On every court

  1. Arrive 10 minutes early. Your slot ends on time so the next group can start.
  2. Wear non-marking sports shoes. No sandals, flip-flops or bare feet.
  3. Water only on court. Food and other drinks stay in the seating area.
  4. Up to 4 players per court.
  5. Cancel at least 4 hours ahead to avoid a charge.
  6. Children under 12 play with an adult present.
  7. No smoking or vaping. Keep music down and be kind to the neighbours.
  8. If lightning or heavy rain stops play, we move your slot or credit your booking.

Pickleball basics

  1. Games go to 11 points, and you win by 2.
  2. Serve underhand and diagonally. The ball must clear the net and the kitchen line.
  3. Two-bounce rule: the serve and the return must each bounce before anyone volleys.
  4. No volleying in the kitchen, the 7 ft zone on each side of the net. The line counts as kitchen.
  5. Only the serving side scores a point.

Tennis etiquette

  1. Court time starts on the hour and includes your warm-up.
  2. Wait for a point to finish before walking behind another court.
  3. Call your opponent's ball honestly. If you are unsure, it is good.
  4. Return a stray ball once the point is over.
  5. Singles use the inner lines. Doubles use the full court.
  6. Collect your balls and rubbish when you finish.
